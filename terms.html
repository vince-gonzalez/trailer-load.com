Facility Dashboards

Any player with an account can start a crew. A free crew holds up to 9 members plus the player who runs it. Whoever runs a crew can see in the game the results of players who joined it and chose to share, and can read each shared session's full saved record, including its timed move log. A license adds the program dashboard and CSV export, facility logins, a signed data agreement, purchase orders and invoices, and support, and removes the member limit. Licenses are sold to schools, training programs, and employers under a separate order or agreement, and if F‑Keys issues a facility login, it goes to the facility. A crew or facility can view a player's results only while the player is in it, and only what the player's sharing box opens to it (joining a crew after its confirmation card turns sharing on). The Privacy Policy says which sessions and walls that covers. Crew owners and facilities agree to use the data solely for training, instruction, and workforce-readiness purposes, to comply with applicable student-data-privacy and employment laws, and not to use the data to unlawfully discriminate. Pricing, term, and data-handling commitments are set out in the applicable order or agreement.
