THE SHORT VERSION

Playing LOCK IN is free, and you can play without giving us anything about yourself. We only save your loading stats if you turn that on, which creating an account does. We only show your stats to a school or employer if you join their crew and confirm it. Signing a wall on the arcade board is your choice too. You can see, export, or delete your data anytime by emailing us. We do not sell personal data.

Who We Are

LOCK IN is a freight-loading training simulator operated by F‑Keys Creative LLC, a Florida limited liability company, and made available at www.trailer-load.com. This Policy explains what information the game and this website collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the LOCK IN game, this website, and the facility dashboard we offer to schools and employers.

What We Collect

Gameplay data · only if you opt in

When you choose to save your stats, the game records information about how you load trailers: pieces placed and their positions, rotations, shelves locked, recoveries, collapses, hazmat handling, water/hydration breaks, tire-nesting actions, score, pace (pieces per hour), wall integrity, session length, and a per-session summary of these figures. This is training-performance data about your gameplay, not about your identity.

Account information · only if you create an account

If you create a worker account to keep your record across devices, we collect a username you choose and a password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider · we never see it). No email address is required, and the game never asks for one. When you create an account we show you a one-time recovery code; we keep only a scrambled (hashed) copy, and it is how you reset a forgotten password. Creating an account is optional and free.

Crew join code · only if you join a crew

If your school or employer gives you a crew join code and you join, we store that code with your account so the crew's dashboard can recognize your shared results. Leaving the crew removes it.

Arcade board initials · only if you sign a wall

After you finish a wall you can sign it with three initials, with no account. We keep the initials, the mode, that wall's score, integrity, pieces and pack, and the date. Nothing else is attached: no account, device, or IP address. Pick initials that are not your full name.

Device & technical data

Our hosting and content-delivery provider (Cloudflare) may process limited technical data such as browser type, approximate region, and pages requested, as an ordinary part of serving and securing the site. We do not run any analytics or tracking service on top of that. The only counting we do is a set of anonymous daily totals, described in Cookies & Analytics.

We never collect: a student email or phone number (accounts use a username only), your real name, location beyond approximate region, payment card numbers (handled by our payment processor for facility purchases, never by us), or any biometric data.

Consent & Your Choices · Double Opt-In

LOCK IN uses a double opt-in model. There are two separate, independent switches:

  • Save my stats. Off by default. Creating an account turns it on, because keeping your record is what an account is for. Your sessions are then saved (locally on your device and to your account). Leave it off and nothing about you or your gameplay is saved: no session, no wall, no score. The only thing that leaves your device is the anonymous daily totals described in Cookies & Analytics.
  • Share with my crew. Off by default. Joining a crew turns it on for that crew, after a confirmation screen that says exactly what the crew will see. The crew's dashboard then shows your shared sessions and walls.

You can change either switch at any time from the in-game ACCOUNT & DATA panel (pause → ADMIN). Leaving the crew, or turning sharing off, hides your walls from it and keeps new sessions private.

Students & Minors

Read this if the player is under 18. LOCK IN is offered to vocational and career-technical-education (CTE) programs, trade schools, and employers. Some players may be minors.

Under 13. LOCK IN is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information (such as email) from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it. Children under 13 may still play the game without saving any data.

Ages 13-17 (students). Where LOCK IN is used inside a school or CTE program, the school may act as the party that provides notice to, and obtains consent from, students and their parents or guardians under applicable law, including the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and state student-data-privacy laws. Where a school directs use of LOCK IN, we act as a service provider and use student gameplay data only to provide and improve the service for that school, and not for our own advertising. We do not use student personal information to serve targeted advertising, and we do not sell student data.

Schools and educators: if you intend to deploy LOCK IN with minors, contact us first so we can put the appropriate data-protection terms in place. We recommend you obtain parent/guardian consent consistent with your institution's policies before students create accounts or share data.

How We Use Data

  • To run the game and show you your own stats and rating.
  • To keep your record across devices (if you have an account).
  • To populate a crew dashboard with the sessions and walls you have chosen to share.
  • To understand, in aggregate and de-identified form, how the training is used so we can improve it, including the anonymous daily totals described below.
  • With your permission only: to show your gameplay statistics in demonstrations of the dashboard to schools and employers, under a pseudonym such as student-7f3, never your username. We ask before including anyone, and you can withdraw at any time by emailing us.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use saved gameplay data to target advertising to you.

Sharing

We share personal information only in these limited cases:

  • On the public leaderboards · if you save your stats, your chosen username and your scores can appear on the public boards (today's daily wall, the all-time records and the season boards), and other players can follow your username. Usernames need not be your real name. To stay off the boards, play without saving stats, or email us and we will remove you.
  • On the arcade board · if you sign a wall, your three initials and that wall's score are shown publicly.
  • With your crew · only the sessions and walls you have opted to share, and only with the crew you joined.
  • In demonstrations, with your permission · under a pseudonym, never your username, as described above.
  • With service providers who run our infrastructure (for example, our database/authentication host and our website host) under contracts that limit their use of the data to providing the service.
  • For legal reasons · if required by law, or to protect rights, safety, or the integrity of the service.
  • In a business transfer · if the Service or its operator is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, with notice to you.

Cookies & Analytics

We do not use Google Analytics or any other third-party analytics or tracking service. There is no advertising and no ad technology of any kind on this site. The only cookies-equivalent storage we use is your own browser’s local storage, which keeps your settings, your training progress, and (if you turn it on) your sign-in · all on your device. Our front page also keeps one aggregate total, visits. It is a single integer on our server · your browser increments it once per tab session, and we store one flag in your browser’s sessionStorage (cleared when you close the tab, never transmitted) so that a page refresh doesn’t double-count. No row, cookie, IP address, or fingerprint is recorded for a visit. The total contains no personal data.

The game also keeps a few more daily totals: how many times it was opened, how many walls were started and finished in each mode, how many daily walls were submitted, how many players read the briefing, and how many walls were signed on the arcade board. Each is one number per day. Nothing about you is attached: no account, device, cookie, or IP address. Whether a visit counts as new or returning is decided on your own device, which keeps a single "1" in local storage to remember it has been here; only which total to add 1 to is sent. These totals are private and are used only to see what in the game is working.

Data Retention

If you opt to save stats, we keep your session records for as long as your account is active or until you ask us to delete them. Local copies live in your browser until you clear them. Facility-shared records are retained according to the facility's agreement with us. We delete or de-identify data we no longer need.

Security

Your saved data is stored on a managed database with row-level security so that, by default, no one can read another person's records. Crew dashboards can only read the sessions and walls of players who joined that crew and agreed to share. Passwords are managed by our authentication provider and stored only as secure hashes. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and to limit what is collected in the first place.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these, email us (below). You can also turn off saving or sharing at any time in the game, and you can delete locally stored data by clearing your browser. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

International Users

LOCK IN is operated from the United States. If you use it from outside the U.S., you understand your information may be processed in the U.S. Where required (for example, for users in the EU/UK), we rely on appropriate safeguards and process data only on the lawful bases of consent and legitimate interest in providing the service.
