THE SHORT VERSION

Playing LOCK IN is free, and you can play without giving us anything about yourself. We only save your loading stats if SAVE MY STATS is on and you are signed in. Creating an account turns it on, and so can signing in (see Consent). We only show your stats to a crew if you join it and confirm. Any player with an account can start a crew, so know whose code you are joining. Signing a wall on the arcade board is your choice too. It puts three initials and that wall's numbers on a public board, never your username. With SAVE MY STATS on, your username and best scores also appear on the public records and Smalls Sort boards. You can see, export, or delete your data at any time by emailing us. We do not sell personal data.

Who We Are

LOCK IN is a freight-loading training simulator operated by F‑Keys Creative LLC, a Florida limited liability company, and made available at www.trailer-load.com. This Policy explains what information the game and this website collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the LOCK IN game, this website, and the program dashboard used by crews, schools and employers.

What We Collect

Gameplay data · only if you opt in

When SAVE MY STATS is on and you are signed in, the game saves information about how you load trailers: pieces placed and their positions, rotations, shelves locked, recoveries, collapses, hazmat handling, water/hydration breaks, tire-nesting actions, lifts, rejected drops, safety warnings, incidents and in-game write-ups (with the rule broken), flags for help, density, the game mode, score, pace (pieces per hour), wall integrity, session length, and a per-session summary of these figures. Each saved session also carries a timed log of your moves (up to 6,000 per session, timed to a tenth of a second), its start and end times, the app version, your two switch settings, the crew code on your device if there is one, and a random ID kept on your device. Signing out deletes that ID from the device, and the game then makes a new one. Each sealed wall, each daily score, and each Smalls Sort run is saved too. This data is about how you play, and it is saved to your account.

Account information · only if you create an account

If you create a worker account to keep your record across devices, we collect a username you choose and a password (stored only as a bcrypt hash · we never store or display it in readable form). No email address is required, and the game does not ask for one or send one with your sessions. Earlier versions sent an email address with each saved session: one typed into an optional box, or an address made from your username. We are deleting these. To have yours removed now, email privacy@trailer-load.com. When you create an account we show you a recovery code. It resets your password if you forget it. We keep only a scrambled (hashed) copy, and the device you signed up on keeps the code until you sign out there. Keep it private: anyone with your username and the code can set a new password. Facility logins for paid dashboards are issued by us and use the instructor's email address. Creating an account is optional and free.

Crews · only if you start or join one

Any player with an account can start a crew and give out its join code. If you start one, we keep its name, its join code, and your account as the one who runs it. If you join one, after a confirmation card that names the crew and lists what it will see, we store the code with your account so whoever runs the crew sees your username and the results you share (see Sharing). Leaving the crew removes the code from your account. Sessions you saved while in it keep the code they were saved with, and the crew can no longer see them. Sessions that older versions sent without an account, with a crew code and sharing on, appear on that crew's dashboard as guest-1, guest-2 and so on, by device ID.

Arcade board initials · only if you sign a wall

After you finish a wall you can sign it with three initials. No account is needed. We keep the initials, the mode, that wall's score, integrity, pieces and pack, and the date and exact time you signed. If you are signed in with SAVE MY STATS on, we also keep which account signed, so your profile can list your initials under "Your initials on the board". Only you see that list. The board never shows it, and no other player, crew, or instructor can see it. As a guest, or with SAVE MY STATS off, nothing else is kept with your initials: no account, device, or IP address. Pick letters that do not identify you.

Device & technical data

Our database and sign-in provider (Supabase), our website host (GitHub Pages), and our content-delivery and security provider (Cloudflare) may process limited technical data such as IP address, browser type, approximate region, and pages requested, as an ordinary part of serving and securing the site, and may keep it in their service logs. Cloudflare also sends Network Error Logging headers, which ask your browser to report failed page loads to Cloudflare. We add no analytics or tracking script of our own. The only counting we do is a set of anonymous daily totals, described in Cookies & Analytics.

We do not collect: a student email or phone number (accounts use a username only), your real name, location beyond approximate region, payment card numbers (handled by our payment processor for facility purchases, never by us), or any biometric data.

Consent & Your Choices · Two Switches

There are two switches. Crew sharing needs both, and a crew's confirmation card turns both on when you join.

  • SAVE MY STATS. The box reads "Save my loading stats (my record)." Off by default. Creating an account turns it on, because keeping your record is what an account is for. Joining a crew turns it on too. With it on and signed in, your sessions, sealed walls and daily scores are saved to your account, and walls you sign are linked to it. With it off, or as a guest, none of that is saved. Your device keeps your personal bests and daily streak either way.
  • The sharing box. It reads "Share my stats with my crew." Off by default, and it works only with SAVE MY STATS on. Joining a crew turns it on, after a confirmation card that names the crew and lists what it will see. Whoever runs the crew then sees your username with the sessions and walls you share.

Both boxes are in the in-game ACCOUNT & DATA panel (pause → ADMIN), and you can change either at any time. While you are in a crew, it sees every session you saved with the sharing box on, including sessions from before you joined it and sessions you shared with an earlier crew. Your walls follow your most recent saved session: while that session has sharing on, the crew sees every wall saved to your account since you joined. Turning sharing off keeps new sessions from the crew, and once a session is saved with sharing off, the crew stops seeing your walls. Sessions you already shared stay visible until you leave the crew. Leaving the crew hides everything from it.

Signing in and shared computers. Signing in turns SAVE MY STATS on only if no choice has been saved on that device. If it was saved off there, it stays off, and the sign-in message says whether it is on or off. The choice is kept on the device, not on your account, and signing out keeps it. Signing out turns sharing off on that device, but you stay in your crew; tick the sharing box again to keep sharing. On a shared computer, check both boxes after you sign in. Apart from a wall you sign, the only gameplay data that leaves your device with SAVE MY STATS off is the anonymous daily totals described in Cookies & Analytics.

Students & Minors

Read this if the player is under 18. LOCK IN is offered to vocational and career-technical-education (CTE) programs, trade schools, and employers. Some players may be minors.

Under 13. LOCK IN is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information (such as email) from children under 13. Creating an account does not ask your age. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it. Children under 13 may still play as guests. If they sign a wall, we keep only what any guest signature keeps: the initials, the mode, that wall's numbers, and the date and time.

Ages 13-17 (students). Where LOCK IN is used inside a school or CTE program, the school may act as the party that provides notice to, and obtains consent from, students and their parents or guardians under applicable law, including the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and state student-data-privacy laws. Where a school directs use of LOCK IN, we act as a service provider and use student gameplay data only to provide and improve the service for that school, and not for our own advertising. The one exception is a dashboard demonstration (see How We Use Data), and a student in a school program is included in one only with the school's written agreement. We do not use student personal information to serve targeted advertising, and we do not sell student data.

Schools and educators: if you intend to deploy LOCK IN with minors, contact us first so we can put the appropriate data-protection terms in place. We recommend you obtain parent/guardian consent consistent with your institution's policies before students create accounts or share data.

How We Use Data

  • To run the game and show you your own stats and rating.
  • To keep your record across devices (if you have an account).
  • To populate a crew dashboard with the sessions and walls you have chosen to share.
  • To understand, in aggregate and de-identified form, how the training is used so we can improve it, including the anonymous daily totals described below.
  • With your permission only: to show your gameplay statistics in demonstrations of the dashboard to schools and employers, under a pseudonym such as student-7f3, never your username. We include a player only after they give us permission, and we record it on their account. For a student in a school program, we also need the school's written agreement. You can withdraw at any time by emailing us.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use saved gameplay data to target advertising to you.

Sharing

We share personal information only in these limited cases:

  • On the public leaderboards · if SAVE MY STATS is on, your chosen username and your scores can appear on the public records boards (best wall, total loaded, fastest wall, cleanest wall, and fewest pieces) and on the Smalls Sort board. Any signed-in player can follow a username, which also tells them whether that username exists, and their friends board then shows that player's username and best score. We store who you follow, and only you can see that list. The player you follow is not told. The daily and arcade boards show initials only. Usernames need not be your real name. Joining a crew turns SAVE MY STATS on, so a player in a crew also appears on the public records and Smalls Sort boards under their username. To stay off those boards, play with SAVE MY STATS off, or email us and we will remove you.
  • On the arcade board · if you sign a wall, your three initials and that wall's score, integrity and date are public. The board never shows a username or which account signed.
  • With your crew · only the crew you joined, and only what you share with it (see Consent for which sessions and walls that covers). Whoever runs the crew sees your username, the dates you made your account and joined the crew, and, for your shared sessions and walls, figures such as score, pace, integrity, density, safety incidents, collapses, water breaks, rotations, lifts, when you last played, and the app version. They see this in the game and on the program dashboard, and they can export it to a CSV file. Through our data API they can also read each shared session's full saved record, including the timed move log and the random device ID. An exported file leaves LOCK IN; deleting your data with us does not delete their copy.
  • In demonstrations, with your permission · under a pseudonym, never your username, as described above.
  • With service providers who run our infrastructure: Supabase for our database and sign-in, GitHub Pages for this website, and Cloudflare for content delivery and security.
  • For legal reasons · if required by law, or to protect rights, safety, or the integrity of the service.
  • In a business transfer · if the Service or its operator is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, with notice to you.

Cookies & Analytics

We do not use Google Analytics, and we add no analytics, advertising or tracking script of our own. There is no advertising and no ad technology of any kind on this site. The only cookies-equivalent storage we use is your own browser’s local storage, which keeps your settings, your personal bests and daily streak, your SAVE MY STATS choice, a random device ID, the last initials you signed with, and, while you are signed in, your sign-in, your username, your crew code and, if you created your account on that device, your recovery code · all on your device. Signing out on any LOCK IN page (the game, your profile, Smalls Sort, or the program dashboard) signs you out of all of them on that device, in every open tab, and asks our server to end that session. It also clears the remembered initials, the username, the recovery code, the crew code and sharing setting, and the device ID. Your SAVE MY STATS choice stays. Our front page also keeps one aggregate total, visits. It is a single integer on our server · your browser increments it once per tab session, and we store one flag in your browser’s sessionStorage (cleared when you close the tab, never transmitted) so that a page refresh doesn’t double-count. The game keeps a few other tab-only flags there too, such as your level bests for that tab. We record no row, cookie, IP address, or fingerprint for a visit. The total contains no personal data.

The game also keeps a few more daily totals: how many times it was opened, how many games were started in each mode, how many walls were finished in the daily and shift modes, how many daily walls were submitted, how many times the briefing was finished, and how many walls were signed on the arcade board. Each is one number per day. Nothing about you is attached: no account, device, cookie, or IP address. Whether a visit counts as new or returning is decided on your own device, which keeps a single "1" in local storage to remember it has been here; only which total to add 1 to is sent. These totals are private and are used only to see what in the game is working.

Data Retention

If SAVE MY STATS is on, we keep your session, wall and daily records until you or your school asks us to delete them. Accounts do not expire. The game no longer keeps copies of your sessions on your device, and it deletes copies that older versions kept the next time you save a session or sign out. Settings, personal bests and your streak stay in your browser until you clear them. Records you shared with a crew can stay visible to it until you leave the crew; for a paid facility dashboard, the facility's agreement with us also applies. We keep signed initials and their numbers. They show on that day's board and can show on the all-time board. If you delete your account, the link between your signatures and your account is removed, and the initials stay. We delete or de-identify data we no longer need.

Security

Your saved data is stored on a managed database with row-level security. Other players cannot read your saved records; they see only what the public boards show (see Sharing). Whoever runs a crew you joined can read your username, the dates you made your account and joined the crew, and the results you shared with it, as Sharing describes. The list of walls you signed can be read only by your own signed-in account, and crews cannot see it. Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes; we never store or display them in readable form. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and to limit what is collected in the first place. If we learn that saved data was accessed without permission, we will tell the affected schools and facilities and post a notice in the game.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these, email us (below). Accounts have no email address, so before we act on a request for a username we confirm it is yours: include the recovery code shown when you made the account, or send the request through your school. You can also turn off saving or sharing at any time in the game, and you can delete locally stored data by clearing your browser. Turning SAVE MY STATS off stops new links between walls you sign and your account. It does not remove links already made. To have those removed, email us. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

International Users

LOCK IN is operated from the United States, and saved records are stored in the United States. If you use it from outside the U.S., you understand your information may be processed in the U.S. Where required (for example, for users in the EU/UK), we rely on appropriate safeguards and process data only on the lawful bases of consent and legitimate interest in providing the service.
