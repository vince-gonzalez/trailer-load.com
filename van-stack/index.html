PRE-LOAD MEETING

  1. Pull. Grab each piece off the belt before it rides past. Missed pieces drop in the pool, and a full pool stops the belt.
  2. Set it in its section. The sticker number is the section. Boxes come in different sizes; build from the shelf up, nothing floats.
  3. Scan it. The scan hands the piece to the driver. No scan, no out-for-delivery.
  4. Smalls go in the bin at the end of their shelf, one scan each.
  5. NCs go on the floor under their section. Special handling (heavy, too long, tires, hazmat) comes on the NC cart, not the belt.
  6. Keep the walkway clear. The painted lines on the floor stay open, always.
  7. Box won't fit its shelf? Set it on the floor under its section instead. That's still the right place.
  8. Room left at the end? Extra freight keeps coming while it fits. Every piece you fit is a bonus.
  9. Dispatch when your scans are done. The driver checks the van in route order: 1, then 2, then the rest.

Controls: click to pick up, set and scan. Green shows where a piece fits; red won't take it. Drag a placed piece to move it (its scan stays good). Press the Space bar to step into the van for the back shelves, and Space again to step back. Esc puts back what you're holding.
