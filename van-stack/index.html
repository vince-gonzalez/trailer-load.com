PRE-LOAD MEETING

  1. Pull. Click a box on the belt. Boxes ride left to right.
  2. Set. Read the sticker, then click that section in the van. The box goes into the next open spot.
  3. Scan. Click the box on the shelf. The scan hands it to the driver. No scan, no out-for-delivery.
  4. Smalls go in the bins. Envelopes and paks go in the bin at the end of their shelf, then click the bin to scan each one. The driver takes the bins up front.
  5. Fix a misload. Drag a box (or a small out of a bin) to pick it back up, then set it where it belongs. Its scan stays good.
  6. Step in. Press Space to climb into the van and work the back shelves up close.
  7. Don't let it ride. Missed pieces fall in the pool. A full pool stops the belt.
  8. Dispatch. When the belt is clear and your scans are done, press Dispatch. The driver walks 1000, then 2000, then the rest.
