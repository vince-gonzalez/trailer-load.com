What it is

The same wall, everywhere

The daily is seeded from the date. Every player gets the same freight in the same order, so two people who played today can compare the same decisions rather than two different piles of luck.

A real trade, as a puzzle

The rules are the ones a freight dock actually runs on: support under every box, dangerous goods low, nothing heavy on a fragile, and a full course locks the wall. Getting it wrong has the consequence it has in the building.

For instructors

A single link you can post Monday morning. It is the same board for the whole class, it runs in the browser, and playing it needs no install and no account.

  • Spatial reasoning. Fitting irregular shapes into a fixed 17 by 20 bay with no undo that is free.
  • Planning under constraint. Ten pieces are visible. Reaching past the front one costs you.
  • Sequencing. A full row locks and protects everything above it, so when you close a course matters more than how fast you fill it.
  • Regulatory reasoning. Dangerous goods load low and stay separated, drawn from 49 CFR.
  • Working to a standard. Pace is measured against 250 pieces per hour, the real hub number.
